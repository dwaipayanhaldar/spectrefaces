Extended Yale Face Database B (Cropped) attribution

This non-commercial educational visualization uses the Extended Yale Face
Database B, Cropped (38 subjects, ~64 frontal illuminations/subject,
pre-cropped and pre-aligned to 192x168 by the database's original curators)
to derive an average face and an ECA (Hadamard-rotated PCA) basis. Images
were resized to 128x128 grayscale; the displayed artifacts -- mean face,
basis images, and the reconstructed example face -- are derived, not
original photographs. The reconstructed example face is a held-out image
from the same public dataset (see manifest.json -> portraitAlignment
.heldOutImage), not a personal photo.

Dataset: http://vision.ucsd.edu/~leekc/ExtYaleDatabase/ExtYaleB.html
(official host has been unreliable; a common mirror of the same published
CroppedYale files is https://github.com/corehello/RPCA)

Citation:
A. Georghiades, P. Belhumeur, D. Kriegman, "From Few to Many: Illumination
Cone Models for Face Recognition under Variable Lighting and Pose," IEEE
Trans. Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 2001.

K.C. Lee, J. Ho, D. Kriegman, "Acquiring Linear Subspaces for Face
Recognition under Variable Lighting," IEEE Trans. Pattern Analysis and
Machine Intelligence, 2005 (extended the database to 38 subjects).

The Extended Yale Face Database B is made available by Yale University for
non-commercial research and educational use. The complete list of source
images used to fit this basis is produced by the offline build pipeline
(scripts/build_eigenfaces_yaleb.py) and retained outside the public web
bundle.
